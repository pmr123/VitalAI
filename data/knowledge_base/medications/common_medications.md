# Common Medications Reference

**DISCLAIMER:** This information is for educational purposes only. Always consult your healthcare provider or pharmacist for medical advice about medications.

## Diabetes Medications

### Metformin (Glucophage)
- **Used for:** Type 2 diabetes, PCOS
- **How it works:** Reduces glucose production in liver, improves insulin sensitivity
- **Common dosage:** 500-2000mg daily, with meals
- **Common side effects:** GI upset, nausea, diarrhea (usually improves over time)
- **Important notes:**
  - Take with food to reduce stomach upset
  - Avoid excessive alcohol
  - May need to stop before medical procedures with contrast dye
  - Does NOT cause low blood sugar when used alone

### Insulin (Various Types)
- **Used for:** Type 1 and Type 2 diabetes
- **Types:** Rapid-acting, short-acting, intermediate, long-acting
- **Important notes:**
  - Rotate injection sites
  - Monitor blood sugar regularly
  - Know signs of low blood sugar
  - Store properly (refrigerate unopened, room temp when in use)

## Blood Pressure Medications

### Lisinopril (Prinivil, Zestril)
- **Used for:** High blood pressure, heart failure
- **Class:** ACE inhibitor
- **Common dosage:** 5-40mg once daily
- **Common side effects:** Dry cough, dizziness, headache
- **Important notes:**
  - May cause persistent dry cough
  - Avoid potassium supplements without doctor approval
  - Can cause dizziness when standing up quickly
  - Do not use during pregnancy

### Amlodipine (Norvasc)
- **Used for:** High blood pressure, angina
- **Class:** Calcium channel blocker
- **Common dosage:** 5-10mg once daily
- **Common side effects:** Ankle swelling, flushing, headache
- **Important notes:**
  - May cause swelling in ankles/feet
  - Avoid grapefruit juice
  - Takes 1-2 weeks for full effect

### Losartan (Cozaar)
- **Used for:** High blood pressure, diabetic kidney protection
- **Class:** ARB (Angiotensin Receptor Blocker)
- **Common dosage:** 25-100mg daily
- **Common side effects:** Dizziness, upper respiratory infection
- **Important notes:**
  - Alternative if ACE inhibitor causes cough
  - Avoid potassium supplements
  - Do not use during pregnancy

## Cholesterol Medications

### Atorvastatin (Lipitor)
- **Used for:** High cholesterol, heart disease prevention
- **Class:** Statin
- **Common dosage:** 10-80mg once daily
- **Common side effects:** Muscle aches, headache, GI upset
- **Important notes:**
  - Take in evening for best effect
  - Report unexplained muscle pain immediately
  - Avoid excessive grapefruit
  - Regular liver function monitoring

### Rosuvastatin (Crestor)
- **Used for:** High cholesterol
- **Class:** Statin
- **Common dosage:** 5-40mg once daily
- **Common side effects:** Similar to atorvastatin
- **Important notes:**
  - Can be taken any time of day
  - Generally fewer drug interactions than other statins

## Pain and Inflammation

### Ibuprofen (Advil, Motrin)
- **Used for:** Pain, inflammation, fever
- **Class:** NSAID
- **Common dosage:** 200-800mg every 4-6 hours as needed
- **Common side effects:** Stomach upset, heartburn
- **Important notes:**
  - Take with food
  - Avoid long-term use without doctor supervision
  - Can increase blood pressure
  - Avoid if kidney problems
  - Can interact with blood pressure medications

### Acetaminophen (Tylenol)
- **Used for:** Pain, fever
- **Common dosage:** 325-1000mg every 4-6 hours (max 3000-4000mg/day)
- **Common side effects:** Generally well-tolerated
- **Important notes:**
  - Safer for kidneys than NSAIDs
  - Watch total daily dose (liver risk)
  - Found in many combination products - don't double up
  - Avoid with heavy alcohol use

## Thyroid Medications

### Levothyroxine (Synthroid)
- **Used for:** Hypothyroidism (underactive thyroid)
- **Common dosage:** 25-200mcg daily
- **Common side effects:** Usually well-tolerated at correct dose
- **Important notes:**
  - Take on empty stomach, 30-60 min before breakfast
  - Take 4 hours apart from calcium, iron, antacids
  - Consistent timing important
  - Regular blood tests to check levels

## Mental Health

### Sertraline (Zoloft)
- **Used for:** Depression, anxiety, OCD, PTSD
- **Class:** SSRI antidepressant
- **Common dosage:** 25-200mg daily
- **Common side effects:** Nausea, headache, sleep changes, sexual side effects
- **Important notes:**
  - Takes 4-6 weeks for full effect
  - Don't stop suddenly
  - May increase anxiety initially
  - Avoid alcohol

### Escitalopram (Lexapro)
- **Used for:** Depression, anxiety
- **Class:** SSRI antidepressant
- **Common dosage:** 10-20mg daily
- **Common side effects:** Similar to sertraline
- **Important notes:**
  - Generally well-tolerated
  - Similar precautions as other SSRIs
