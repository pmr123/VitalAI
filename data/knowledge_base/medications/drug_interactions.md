# Common Drug Interactions

**IMPORTANT:** This is general information only. Always consult your pharmacist or healthcare provider about specific drug interactions for your medications.

## What is a Drug Interaction?
A drug interaction occurs when a medication's effect is changed by:
- Another drug
- Food or drink
- A health condition

Interactions can:
- Make medications less effective
- Increase side effects
- Create dangerous effects
- Change how medications are processed

## Common Interaction Categories

### Blood Thinners + NSAIDs
**Interacting drugs:**
- Warfarin (Coumadin) + Ibuprofen/Aspirin/Naproxen
- Aspirin + other NSAIDs

**Risk:** Increased bleeding risk

**Recommendation:**
- Use acetaminophen (Tylenol) for pain instead
- If NSAID necessary, use lowest dose for shortest time
- Monitor for signs of bleeding
- Inform all healthcare providers

### ACE Inhibitors/ARBs + Potassium
**Interacting drugs:**
- Lisinopril, Losartan + Potassium supplements
- ACE inhibitors + Salt substitutes (often contain potassium)

**Risk:** Dangerously high potassium levels

**Recommendation:**
- Avoid potassium supplements unless prescribed
- Read labels on salt substitutes
- Regular potassium monitoring through blood tests

### Statins + Certain Foods/Drugs
**Interacting substances:**
- Atorvastatin, Simvastatin + Grapefruit juice
- Statins + Certain antibiotics (erythromycin, clarithromycin)
- Statins + Certain antifungals

**Risk:** Increased statin levels → muscle damage risk

**Recommendation:**
- Avoid grapefruit with most statins
- Inform doctors of all medications before new prescriptions
- Report unexplained muscle pain

### Metformin + Contrast Dye
**Interaction:** Metformin + Iodinated contrast (used in CT scans, etc.)

**Risk:** Rare but serious kidney-related complication

**Recommendation:**
- Stop metformin before and 48 hours after procedures with contrast
- Follow specific instructions from your healthcare provider
- Resume after kidney function confirmed normal

### SSRIs + MAOIs
**Interacting drugs:**
- Sertraline, Escitalopram + MAOIs (older antidepressants)

**Risk:** Serotonin syndrome (potentially life-threatening)

**Recommendation:**
- NEVER combine these medications
- Wait appropriate washout period when switching
- Includes some MAOIs in other medications

### Thyroid Medication + Absorption Blockers
**Interacting substances:**
- Levothyroxine + Calcium supplements
- Levothyroxine + Iron supplements
- Levothyroxine + Antacids
- Levothyroxine + Coffee

**Risk:** Reduced absorption of thyroid medication

**Recommendation:**
- Take levothyroxine alone, on empty stomach
- Wait 4 hours before taking calcium, iron, or antacids
- Wait 30-60 minutes before coffee or food

### Blood Pressure Meds + NSAIDs
**Interacting drugs:**
- ACE inhibitors, ARBs, diuretics + Ibuprofen/Naproxen

**Risk:** Reduced blood pressure medication effectiveness, kidney stress

**Recommendation:**
- Use acetaminophen when possible
- Short-term NSAID use only if necessary
- Monitor blood pressure

## Food and Beverage Interactions

### Grapefruit
**Affects:** Many medications including statins, some blood pressure meds, some psychiatric medications

**Mechanism:** Blocks enzyme that breaks down medications → higher drug levels

**Recommendation:** Check if your specific medication interacts; avoid grapefruit if it does

### Alcohol
**Affects:** Many medications including:
- Blood pressure medications (additive lowering effect)
- Diabetes medications (blood sugar effects)
- Mental health medications (increased sedation)
- Pain medications (increased side effects)
- Acetaminophen (liver toxicity risk)

**Recommendation:** Discuss alcohol use with your healthcare provider

### Vitamin K-Rich Foods
**Affects:** Warfarin (blood thinner)

**Foods:** Leafy greens (spinach, kale, broccoli)

**Recommendation:** Don't avoid these foods, but keep intake consistent day-to-day

### Tyramine-Rich Foods
**Affects:** MAOIs (certain antidepressants)

**Foods:** Aged cheeses, cured meats, fermented foods

**Risk:** Dangerous blood pressure spikes

## Supplements That Can Interact

### St. John's Wort
- Interacts with MANY medications
- Can reduce effectiveness of birth control, antidepressants, blood thinners
- Always disclose to healthcare providers

### Ginkgo Biloba
- May increase bleeding risk
- Interacts with blood thinners, NSAIDs

### Fish Oil (High Doses)
- May increase bleeding risk at high doses
- Can interact with blood thinners

### Calcium/Iron
- Affect absorption of many medications
- Take at different times of day

## Protecting Yourself

### Do's
- Keep a complete list of all medications and supplements
- Use one pharmacy when possible (they track interactions)
- Ask pharmacist about interactions with new medications
- Tell all healthcare providers about everything you take
- Read medication guides and labels
- Ask before taking OTC medications

### Don'ts
- Start new supplements without checking interactions
- Assume "natural" means safe
- Skip telling doctors about OTC medications
- Stop medications without consulting provider
- Share medications with others

## Signs of Dangerous Interactions
Seek immediate medical attention if you experience:
- Unusual bleeding or bruising
- Severe dizziness or fainting
- Difficulty breathing
- Rapid or irregular heartbeat
- Severe skin reactions
- Confusion or altered mental state
- Severe muscle pain or weakness
