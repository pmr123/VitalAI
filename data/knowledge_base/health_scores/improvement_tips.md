# Tips to Improve Your Health Scores

## Quick Reference: Score Improvement Actions

| If Low Score In... | Top 3 Actions |
|-------------------|---------------|
| Cardiac | Exercise, manage stress, sleep better |
| Activity | Walk more, reduce sitting, set step goals |
| Recovery | Fix sleep schedule, limit alcohol, dark room |
| Metabolic | Balanced diet, consistent exercise, manage weight |

## Improving Cardiac Score

### Immediate Actions (This Week)
1. **Add daily walks** - Even 10-15 minutes helps
2. **Practice deep breathing** - 5 minutes, twice daily
3. **Reduce caffeine** - Especially after noon
4. **Hydrate better** - Dehydration affects HRV

### Short-term Actions (This Month)
1. **Start aerobic exercise routine** - 3x/week, 20-30 minutes
2. **Establish stress management practice** - Meditation app, yoga
3. **Optimize sleep** - Consistent times, better environment
4. **Reduce alcohol** - Especially before bed

### Long-term Habits (Ongoing)
1. **Build cardio fitness** - Work up to 150 min/week moderate activity
2. **Maintain healthy weight** - Reduces heart strain
3. **Regular health checkups** - Monitor blood pressure, cholesterol
4. **Manage chronic stress** - Therapy, lifestyle changes if needed

## Improving Activity Score

### Immediate Actions (Today)
1. **Take a walk** - Any duration is better than none
2. **Set hourly movement reminders** - Stand and move every hour
3. **Take stairs** - Skip elevator when possible
4. **Park farther away** - Extra steps add up

### Short-term Actions (This Week)
1. **Set a step goal** - Start with current average + 1,000
2. **Schedule exercise** - Put it in your calendar
3. **Find an activity buddy** - Accountability helps
4. **Try a new activity** - Find what you enjoy

### Long-term Habits (Ongoing)
1. **Build non-negotiable exercise routine** - Same days/times weekly
2. **Active transportation** - Walk or bike when possible
3. **Active hobbies** - Hiking, sports, dance, gardening
4. **Standing desk** - Reduce sitting time at work

### Making Steps Count
| Activity | Approximate Steps |
|----------|-------------------|
| 1 mile walk | 2,000-2,500 |
| 10 min walk | 1,000-1,200 |
| Grocery shopping | 1,500-3,000 |
| Office day (sedentary) | 2,000-3,000 |
| Active day | 8,000-15,000+ |

## Improving Recovery Score

### Immediate Actions (Tonight)
1. **Set a bedtime** - Stick to it tonight
2. **Remove screens** - No phone/TV 1 hour before bed
3. **Cool your room** - 65-68°F (18-20°C) is optimal
4. **Darken your room** - Blackout curtains or eye mask

### Short-term Actions (This Week)
1. **Establish sleep routine** - Same time daily, even weekends
2. **Create wind-down ritual** - Reading, stretching, bath
3. **Limit caffeine** - None after 2pm
4. **Reduce alcohol** - Especially within 3 hours of bed

### Long-term Habits (Ongoing)
1. **Protect your sleep schedule** - Treat it as non-negotiable
2. **Address sleep disorders** - See doctor if issues persist
3. **Manage stress** - Chronic stress ruins sleep
4. **Exercise regularly** - But not too close to bedtime

### Sleep Environment Checklist
- [ ] Room temperature 65-68°F (18-20°C)
- [ ] Complete darkness (or eye mask)
- [ ] Quiet (or white noise machine)
- [ ] Comfortable mattress and pillows
- [ ] No screens in bedroom
- [ ] Phone on do-not-disturb

## Improving Metabolic Score

### Immediate Actions (Today)
1. **Drink water** - Often confused with hunger
2. **Take a walk after meals** - Helps blood sugar
3. **Choose whole foods** - Less processed options
4. **Watch portion sizes** - Use smaller plates

### Short-term Actions (This Month)
1. **Track what you eat** - Awareness is the first step
2. **Add vegetables** - To every meal
3. **Reduce sugar** - Especially sugary drinks
4. **Establish exercise routine** - Helps metabolism

### Long-term Habits (Ongoing)
1. **Sustainable eating pattern** - Not diets, lifestyle
2. **Regular exercise** - Both cardio and strength
3. **Stress management** - Stress affects metabolism
4. **Regular health checkups** - Monitor metabolic markers

### Nutrition Quick Wins
- Swap sugary drinks for water
- Add protein to each meal
- Fill half your plate with vegetables
- Choose whole grains over refined
- Limit ultra-processed foods
- Cook at home more often

## General Health Improvement Framework

### The 1% Rule
Don't try to change everything at once:
- Make one small improvement at a time
- 1% better each day compounds dramatically
- Sustainable beats dramatic

### Priority Order
1. **Sleep** - Foundation for everything else
2. **Movement** - Daily activity baseline
3. **Stress** - Manage what you can control
4. **Nutrition** - Fuel your body well
5. **Weight** - Often follows other improvements

### Tracking Progress
- Check scores weekly, not daily
- Take photos of progress
- Celebrate small wins
- Don't let setbacks derail you
- Focus on the trend, not individual days

### When to Seek Help
Consult a healthcare provider if:
- Scores consistently declining despite efforts
- Any concerning symptoms
- Need help creating a plan
- Underlying health conditions
- Scores extremely low with no explanation
