# Cardio-Metabolic Index (CMI)

## What is the Cardio-Metabolic Index?
The Cardio-Metabolic Index (CMI) is a composite health score that combines multiple health indicators to give you a single, easy-to-understand number representing your overall cardiovascular and metabolic health.

## How CMI is Calculated

CMI in VitalAI combines four component scores:

| Component | Weight | What It Measures |
|-----------|--------|------------------|
| Cardiac Score | 30% | Heart health (resting HR, HRV) |
| Activity Score | 25% | Physical activity level |
| Recovery Score | 25% | Sleep and rest quality |
| Metabolic Score | 20% | Metabolic health (BMI, activity) |

**Formula:**
CMI = (Cardiac × 0.30) + (Activity × 0.25) + (Recovery × 0.25) + (Metabolic × 0.20)

## Understanding Your CMI Score

| Score Range | Rating | Interpretation |
|-------------|--------|----------------|
| 85-100 | Excellent | Outstanding health markers |
| 70-84 | Good | Healthy, room for optimization |
| 50-69 | Fair | Some areas need attention |
| Below 50 | Needs Improvement | Multiple areas to address |

## Component Score Breakdown

### Cardiac Score (30% of CMI)
Measures cardiovascular health through:
- **Resting Heart Rate:** Lower is generally better (60-70 BPM ideal)
- **Heart Rate Variability:** Higher is better (indicates good recovery)

**How to improve:**
- Regular aerobic exercise
- Stress management
- Quality sleep
- Limit caffeine and alcohol

### Activity Score (25% of CMI)
Measures physical activity through:
- **Daily steps** compared to your goal
- **Consistency** of activity
- **Active minutes** in moderate+ intensity

**How to improve:**
- Aim for 7,500-10,000 steps daily
- Get 150+ minutes moderate activity weekly
- Reduce prolonged sitting
- Stay consistent day-to-day

### Recovery Score (25% of CMI)
Measures rest and recovery through:
- **Sleep duration** (optimal: 7-8 hours)
- **Deep sleep** percentage
- **Sleep quality** score

**How to improve:**
- Maintain consistent sleep schedule
- Create dark, cool sleeping environment
- Limit screens before bed
- Avoid alcohol close to bedtime

### Metabolic Score (20% of CMI)
Measures metabolic health through:
- **BMI** (if height/weight available)
- **Activity patterns**
- **Recovery indicators**

**How to improve:**
- Maintain healthy weight
- Regular physical activity
- Balanced nutrition
- Manage stress

## Why CMI Matters

### Benefits of Tracking CMI
1. **Single number simplicity** - Easy to understand at a glance
2. **Holistic view** - Combines multiple health factors
3. **Trend tracking** - See improvement over time
4. **Early warning** - Declining scores may signal issues
5. **Motivation** - Gamifies health improvement

### Limitations
- Not a medical diagnosis
- Individual variation is normal
- Short-term fluctuations are normal
- Should be used alongside, not instead of, medical care

## Improving Your CMI

### Quick Wins (Can improve in days/weeks)
- Get more sleep
- Add a daily walk
- Reduce alcohol
- Practice stress management

### Long-term Improvements (Weeks/months)
- Build consistent exercise habit
- Maintain healthy weight
- Develop good sleep hygiene
- Build cardio fitness

### Track Your Progress
- Check CMI weekly for trends
- Don't obsess over daily fluctuations
- Celebrate improvements
- Address declining trends early

## CMI and Overall Health

Your CMI is correlated with:
- Cardiovascular disease risk
- Metabolic syndrome risk
- Energy levels and vitality
- Mental health and mood
- Longevity indicators

**Remember:** CMI is a tool for awareness and motivation, not a replacement for medical advice. Consult healthcare providers for health concerns.
