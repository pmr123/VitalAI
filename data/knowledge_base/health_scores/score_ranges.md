# Understanding Your Health Score Ranges

## Score Scale Overview
All VitalAI health scores use a 0-100 scale for consistency and easy understanding.

| Range | Label | Color Code | Meaning |
|-------|-------|------------|---------|
| 85-100 | Excellent | Green | Optimal health in this area |
| 70-84 | Good | Blue | Healthy, minor optimization possible |
| 50-69 | Fair | Yellow | Attention needed, room for improvement |
| 0-49 | Needs Work | Red | Significant improvement needed |

## Cardiac Score Details

### What's Measured
- Resting heart rate (RHR)
- Heart rate variability (HRV)

### Score Breakdown
| Score | RHR Range | HRV Range | Meaning |
|-------|-----------|-----------|---------|
| 85-100 | 55-65 BPM | 60+ ms | Excellent cardiovascular fitness |
| 70-84 | 60-72 BPM | 45-60 ms | Good heart health |
| 50-69 | 72-82 BPM | 30-45 ms | Average, could improve |
| 0-49 | 82+ BPM | <30 ms | Needs attention |

### What Affects Cardiac Score
**Positive impact:** Regular exercise, stress management, good sleep, hydration
**Negative impact:** Sedentary lifestyle, chronic stress, poor sleep, alcohol, caffeine

## Activity Score Details

### What's Measured
- Daily steps vs goal
- Step consistency across days
- Active minutes

### Score Breakdown
| Score | Steps (10k goal) | Consistency | Meaning |
|-------|------------------|-------------|---------|
| 85-100 | 10,000+ | Very consistent | Highly active lifestyle |
| 70-84 | 7,500-10,000 | Mostly consistent | Good activity level |
| 50-69 | 5,000-7,500 | Some variation | Moderately active |
| 0-49 | <5,000 | Inconsistent | Sedentary |

### What Affects Activity Score
**Positive impact:** Daily walks, exercise routine, active commute, reducing sitting
**Negative impact:** Sedentary job, no exercise routine, inconsistent activity

## Recovery Score Details

### What's Measured
- Sleep duration
- Deep sleep percentage
- Overall sleep quality

### Score Breakdown
| Score | Sleep Duration | Deep Sleep | Meaning |
|-------|----------------|------------|---------|
| 85-100 | 7-8.5 hours | 1.5+ hours | Excellent recovery |
| 70-84 | 6.5-7 or 8.5-9 hrs | 1-1.5 hours | Good recovery |
| 50-69 | 5.5-6.5 hours | 0.5-1 hour | Inadequate recovery |
| 0-49 | <5.5 hours | <0.5 hour | Poor recovery |

### What Affects Recovery Score
**Positive impact:** Consistent sleep schedule, dark/cool room, limiting alcohol
**Negative impact:** Irregular schedule, screens before bed, alcohol, stress

## Metabolic Score Details

### What's Measured
- BMI (Body Mass Index)
- Activity patterns
- Overall lifestyle factors

### Score Breakdown (BMI component)
| Score | BMI Range | Category |
|-------|-----------|----------|
| 85-100 | 18.5-24.9 | Healthy weight |
| 70-84 | 25-27 | Slightly overweight |
| 50-69 | 27-30 | Overweight |
| 0-49 | <18.5 or >30 | Underweight or Obese |

### What Affects Metabolic Score
**Positive impact:** Healthy weight, regular activity, balanced diet
**Negative impact:** Excess weight, sedentary lifestyle, poor nutrition

## Score Fluctuations

### Normal Day-to-Day Variation
- Scores can vary 5-10 points daily
- This is completely normal
- Focus on weekly/monthly trends, not daily numbers

### When to Pay Attention
- Consistent decline over 1-2 weeks
- Score drops more than 15 points suddenly
- Multiple scores declining together

### What Causes Fluctuations
- Sleep quality variation
- Stress levels
- Illness (even minor)
- Alcohol consumption
- Travel or schedule changes
- Menstrual cycle (for women)

## Using Scores Effectively

### Do's
- Track weekly trends
- Focus on lowest-scoring area first
- Celebrate improvements
- Use as motivation tool
- Discuss concerns with healthcare provider

### Don'ts
- Obsess over daily numbers
- Compare your scores to others
- Use scores for medical diagnosis
- Ignore consistently low scores
- Let scores cause anxiety

## Setting Personal Goals

### Realistic Improvement Targets
- Aim for 5-10 point improvement per month
- Focus on one component at a time
- Build sustainable habits, not quick fixes

### Priority Order for Improvement
1. **Sleep first** - Foundation for all other improvements
2. **Activity second** - Builds on good rest
3. **Stress management** - Improves cardiac metrics
4. **Weight management** - Long-term metabolic health
