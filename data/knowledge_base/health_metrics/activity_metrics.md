# Activity Metrics: Steps, Calories, and Active Minutes

## Daily Steps

### What Counts as a Step?
Wearables detect steps using accelerometers that sense motion patterns characteristic of walking or running.

### Step Goals and Health

| Daily Steps | Category |
|-------------|----------|
| < 5,000 | Sedentary |
| 5,000 - 7,499 | Low active |
| 7,500 - 9,999 | Somewhat active |
| 10,000 - 12,499 | Active |
| > 12,500 | Highly active |

### The 10,000 Steps Myth
- Originated from a 1960s Japanese marketing campaign
- Not scientifically derived, but a reasonable goal
- Research shows benefits plateau around 7,500-10,000 steps
- Some benefit from as few as 4,000 steps vs sedentary
- **Key:** Any increase in steps is beneficial

### Health Benefits of Walking
- Reduced risk of heart disease
- Lower blood pressure
- Better blood sugar control
- Weight management
- Improved mood and mental health
- Stronger bones and muscles

## Calories Burned

### Understanding Calorie Estimates
Wearables estimate calories using:
- **BMR (Basal Metabolic Rate):** Calories burned at rest
- **Activity data:** Steps, heart rate, movement intensity
- **Personal info:** Age, weight, height, gender

### Types of Calorie Burn

**Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE):**
- BMR (60-70% of total)
- Activity calories (20-30%)
- Thermic effect of food (10%)

**Active Calories:**
- Only calories from intentional activity
- Exercise, walking, movement
- This is what most wearables highlight

### Calorie Accuracy
- Wearable estimates can be off by 20-50%
- Heart rate improves accuracy
- Use for trends, not exact numbers
- Compare day-to-day, not absolute values

## Active Minutes

### What Are Active Minutes?
Minutes spent in moderate-to-vigorous physical activity:
- **Moderate:** Brisk walking, light cycling, dancing
  - Can talk but not sing
  - Heart rate 50-70% of max
- **Vigorous:** Running, swimming, intense cycling
  - Difficult to speak more than a few words
  - Heart rate 70-85% of max

### Weekly Activity Guidelines (WHO/CDC)
**Adults (18-64):**
- 150 minutes moderate activity, OR
- 75 minutes vigorous activity, OR
- Equivalent combination
- PLUS muscle strengthening 2+ days/week

**Additional benefits with:**
- 300 minutes moderate, OR
- 150 minutes vigorous

### Breaking Down the Goal
| Daily Target | Weekly Total |
|--------------|--------------|
| ~22 min moderate | 150 min |
| ~11 min vigorous | 75 min |
| 30 min moderate | 210 min (exceeds goal) |

## Activity and Heart Health

### Exercise Heart Rate Zones
Based on Maximum HR (roughly 220 - age):

| Zone | % Max HR | Benefit |
|------|----------|---------|
| Zone 1: Very Light | 50-60% | Recovery, warm-up |
| Zone 2: Light | 60-70% | Fat burning, endurance base |
| Zone 3: Moderate | 70-80% | Aerobic fitness |
| Zone 4: Hard | 80-90% | Performance, speed |
| Zone 5: Maximum | 90-100% | Short bursts only |

### Ideal Weekly Mix
- Mostly Zone 2 (80% of training time)
- Some Zone 3-4 (20% of training time)
- Zone 5 only for specific training

## Tips for Increasing Activity

1. **Start small** - Add 1,000 steps to current baseline
2. **Take walking meetings** - Move while you talk
3. **Use stairs** - Skip elevators when possible
4. **Park farther away** - Extra steps add up
5. **Set hourly reminders** - Stand and move every hour
6. **Find activities you enjoy** - Sustainability matters
7. **Track progress** - Wearables help motivation
8. **Exercise with others** - Social accountability helps

## Movement Throughout the Day

**Sitting is the new smoking:**
- Prolonged sitting increases health risks
- Even if you exercise, long sitting periods are harmful
- Break up sitting every 30-60 minutes

**Strategies:**
- Standing desk or desk converter
- Walk during phone calls
- Stretch breaks
- Walking after meals (helps blood sugar)
