# Heart Rate (HR)

## What is Heart Rate?
Heart rate is the number of times your heart beats per minute (BPM). It's one of the most fundamental indicators of cardiovascular health and overall fitness.

## Normal Heart Rate Ranges

| Age Group | Resting HR (BPM) |
|-----------|------------------|
| Adults (18+) | 60-100 BPM |
| Well-trained athletes | 40-60 BPM |
| Children (6-15) | 70-100 BPM |

## Types of Heart Rate Measurements

### Resting Heart Rate (RHR)
- Measured when you're calm and haven't been active
- Best measured first thing in the morning
- Lower RHR generally indicates better cardiovascular fitness
- Elite athletes may have RHR of 40-50 BPM

### Active Heart Rate
- Heart rate during physical activity
- Increases with exercise intensity
- Maximum HR roughly estimated as: 220 - your age

### Recovery Heart Rate
- How quickly your HR returns to normal after exercise
- Faster recovery = better cardiovascular fitness
- Healthy: HR should drop 20+ BPM within first minute after stopping exercise

## What Affects Heart Rate?

**Factors that INCREASE heart rate:**
- Physical activity and exercise
- Stress, anxiety, or excitement
- Caffeine and stimulants
- Dehydration
- Fever or illness
- Some medications
- Hot weather

**Factors that DECREASE heart rate:**
- Regular exercise (over time)
- Relaxation and sleep
- Good hydration
- Beta-blocker medications
- High fitness level

## When to Be Concerned

Consult a doctor if you experience:
- Resting HR consistently above 100 BPM (tachycardia)
- Resting HR consistently below 60 BPM with symptoms (bradycardia)
- Irregular heartbeat (arrhythmia)
- Heart rate that doesn't increase with exercise
- Sudden changes in your normal pattern

## How Wearables Measure Heart Rate
Most wearables use photoplethysmography (PPG):
- LED lights shine through your skin
- Sensors detect blood volume changes
- Algorithm calculates heart rate from the pattern
- Accuracy varies with skin tone, fit, and movement

## Tips for Improving Heart Rate Health
1. Regular aerobic exercise (150 min/week moderate, or 75 min/week vigorous)
2. Manage stress through meditation or breathing exercises
3. Stay hydrated
4. Limit caffeine and alcohol
5. Get adequate sleep (7-9 hours)
6. Maintain a healthy weight
