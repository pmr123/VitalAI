# Sleep Stages and Sleep Quality

## The Sleep Cycle
Sleep occurs in cycles of approximately 90 minutes, with each cycle containing different stages. A typical night includes 4-6 complete cycles.

## Sleep Stages Explained

### Stage 1: Light Sleep (N1)
- **Duration:** 1-5 minutes per cycle
- **What happens:** Transition from wakefulness to sleep
- **Brain waves:** Slow down from alpha to theta waves
- **Body:** Muscles relax, easy to wake up
- **Percentage of night:** 5% of total sleep

### Stage 2: Light Sleep (N2)
- **Duration:** 10-25 minutes (increases in later cycles)
- **What happens:** Body temperature drops, heart rate slows
- **Brain waves:** Sleep spindles and K-complexes appear
- **Body:** Further relaxation, harder to wake
- **Percentage of night:** 45-55% of total sleep

### Stage 3: Deep Sleep (N3/Slow-Wave Sleep)
- **Duration:** 20-40 minutes (mostly in first half of night)
- **What happens:** Most restorative stage
- **Brain waves:** Slow delta waves dominate
- **Body:** Very difficult to wake, physical restoration occurs
- **Percentage of night:** 15-20% of total sleep
- **Benefits:**
  - Physical recovery and repair
  - Immune system strengthening
  - Growth hormone release
  - Memory consolidation

### REM Sleep (Rapid Eye Movement)
- **Duration:** 10-60 minutes (increases in later cycles)
- **What happens:** Vivid dreams occur
- **Brain waves:** Similar to wakefulness
- **Body:** Temporary paralysis (prevents acting out dreams)
- **Percentage of night:** 20-25% of total sleep
- **Benefits:**
  - Emotional processing
  - Memory consolidation
  - Learning and creativity
  - Brain restoration

## Ideal Sleep Distribution

For a healthy adult (7-8 hours sleep):
| Stage | Ideal % | Hours |
|-------|---------|-------|
| Light (N1+N2) | 50-60% | 3.5-4.5 hrs |
| Deep (N3) | 15-20% | 1-1.5 hrs |
| REM | 20-25% | 1.5-2 hrs |

## Sleep Quality Indicators

### Good Sleep Quality
- Fall asleep within 15-20 minutes
- Sleep through the night (or wake briefly <1 time)
- Adequate deep sleep (>1 hour)
- Adequate REM sleep (>1.5 hours)
- Feel refreshed upon waking

### Poor Sleep Quality Signs
- Taking >30 minutes to fall asleep
- Waking frequently during the night
- Very little deep sleep (<45 min)
- Very little REM sleep (<1 hour)
- Feeling tired despite adequate hours

## How Wearables Track Sleep
Wearables use combinations of:
- **Movement (accelerometer):** Less movement = deeper sleep
- **Heart rate patterns:** Lower, more stable = deep sleep
- **HRV patterns:** Characteristic changes per stage
- **Algorithms:** Estimate stages from sensor data

**Accuracy note:** Consumer wearables are ~70-80% accurate compared to clinical polysomnography. Good for trends, not perfect for diagnostics.

## Factors Affecting Sleep Quality

**Negative factors:**
- Caffeine (avoid 6+ hours before bed)
- Alcohol (disrupts REM sleep)
- Blue light from screens
- Irregular sleep schedule
- Stress and anxiety
- Heavy meals before bed
- Hot sleeping environment

**Positive factors:**
- Consistent sleep/wake times
- Cool, dark bedroom (65-68°F / 18-20°C)
- Limited screen time before bed
- Regular exercise (not too close to bedtime)
- Relaxation routine before bed

## Tips for Better Sleep
1. **Maintain consistent times** - Same bedtime and wake time daily
2. **Create a sleep sanctuary** - Dark, cool, quiet room
3. **Limit screens** - No screens 1 hour before bed
4. **Watch caffeine** - None after 2pm
5. **Limit alcohol** - Disrupts sleep architecture
6. **Exercise regularly** - But not within 3 hours of bed
7. **Manage stress** - Meditation, journaling, deep breathing
8. **Optimize deep sleep** - Avoid alcohol, keep cool, exercise
