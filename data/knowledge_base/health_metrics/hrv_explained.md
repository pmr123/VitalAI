# Heart Rate Variability (HRV)

## What is HRV?
Heart Rate Variability (HRV) measures the variation in time between consecutive heartbeats. Unlike heart rate which counts beats per minute, HRV looks at the tiny differences between each beat interval, measured in milliseconds (ms).

## Why HRV Matters
HRV is controlled by your autonomic nervous system (ANS), which has two branches:
- **Sympathetic** ("fight or flight") - decreases HRV
- **Parasympathetic** ("rest and digest") - increases HRV

Higher HRV generally indicates:
- Better stress resilience
- Good recovery
- Balanced nervous system
- Overall better health

Lower HRV may indicate:
- Chronic stress
- Poor recovery
- Overtraining
- Illness or fatigue

## Normal HRV Ranges

HRV varies significantly by age and individual:

| Age | Average HRV (RMSSD) |
|-----|---------------------|
| 20-25 | 50-100 ms |
| 25-35 | 45-80 ms |
| 35-45 | 35-65 ms |
| 45-55 | 25-55 ms |
| 55+ | 20-45 ms |

**Important:** Your personal baseline matters more than comparing to others. Track your own trends over time.

## How to Interpret Your HRV

### Higher than your baseline
- Good recovery
- Body is ready for stress/exercise
- Well-rested state

### Lower than your baseline
- Body is under stress
- May need more recovery
- Could indicate:
  - Poor sleep
  - Illness coming on
  - Overtraining
  - Alcohol consumption
  - High stress

## Factors That Affect HRV

**Decrease HRV (negative):**
- Poor sleep
- Alcohol
- Illness
- Overtraining
- Chronic stress
- Dehydration
- Poor nutrition

**Increase HRV (positive):**
- Quality sleep
- Regular exercise (not overtraining)
- Meditation and deep breathing
- Good hydration
- Balanced nutrition
- Cold exposure (like cold showers)

## HRV and Training
Athletes use HRV to optimize training:
- High HRV day → Good day for intense training
- Low HRV day → Consider rest or light activity
- Tracking trends helps prevent overtraining

## How Wearables Measure HRV
- Most devices measure HRV during sleep or rest
- Common metrics: RMSSD, SDNN, LF/HF ratio
- For accuracy, measure at the same time daily (morning is best)
- Chest straps are more accurate than wrist-based optical sensors

## Tips to Improve HRV
1. Prioritize sleep quality (7-9 hours)
2. Practice deep breathing or meditation (5-10 min daily)
3. Exercise regularly but avoid overtraining
4. Limit alcohol, especially before bed
5. Manage chronic stress
6. Stay hydrated
7. Eat a balanced diet rich in omega-3s
